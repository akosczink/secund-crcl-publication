PROJECT SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The CRCL project investigates how artificial intelligence can refine itself
through recursive loops of perception, evaluation, and correction — leading
to measurable cognitive evolution.

Each loop iteration maximizes the Reality–Believability Balance (RBB)
while maintaining zero hallucination (tofu=0).  
The system evolves through self-assessment metrics (CPA, CGAS, CMD, SR),
forming a sustainable self-improvement engine.
